Common questions

When can I enroll in a Marketplace plan?

Open enrollment happens once a year, and special enrollment is available after qualifying life events such as losing coverage, moving or having a baby. Contact us and we will confirm your window.

What is the difference between Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum?

They describe how costs are shared. Bronze has the lowest premium and highest cost when you get care. Platinum is the reverse. Silver and Gold sit in between.

Can you confirm my doctor is in a plan’s network?

Yes. Tell us the doctor, lab or clinic and we will check it with the carrier before you enroll. Networks change each plan year, so we recheck at renewal.

Does it cost extra to use your agency?

No. Your premium is the same whether you buy through us or directly from the carrier.
